THE RULES (Keenan Malloy FBEOD)

1 set per movement pattern — not more, not less.
All sets to failure — push until you can't get another clean rep.
5–10 reps — use a weight in this range. If you hit 10, go heavier next time.
Every other day — train Mon, Wed, Fri (or any EOD pattern).
Progressive overload — log your weights. Beat them next session.
Standardise form first — same ROM every rep, every session.
Machines > free weights for beginners — better isolation, safer failure.
Bilateral movements — two limbs at once while learning.
